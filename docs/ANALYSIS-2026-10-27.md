# Four-lens analysis of main (3ec34b9)

*Snapshot: main at 3ec34b9 (2026-10-27). Main has moved since: for example #84 merged afterwards, so findings about the xweather Location handling may be stale. Produced by Claude Code at the owner's request: 19 read-only analyses, 36 agents in all, with a skeptic pass on the top unexecuted findings. Spot-checked by the author of this PR: the longitude-limit mutation (tested-core-2) reproduces, the skeptic tally (7 confirmed, 9 partly, 0 refuted) matches the run journal, and the main ruleset's required-check list is empty as variety-ci-1 states. The rest of the findings are as the analysts reported them.*


## Summary

- **Several guards that matter are unpinned.** Mutations survived unittest (1078 checks), robust (299,308 inputs) and feature.sh (111). The survivors were:
  - the longitude limit changed from 180 to 90 (tested-core-2);
  - the JSON depth limit (tested-core-1, subtract-core-1, variety-tests-6);
  - case-insensitive Content-Length (tested-core-3);
  - TLS seed injection and entropy refusal (tested-tls-1, subtract-tls-1);
  - the RSA suites (tested-tls-2);
  - Cancelled/Timeout provenance through TLS (tested-tls-3);
  - `commit_preferences` refetch, auto-refresh and field retention (tested-app-1, -2, -3).

  Confidence high, executed.
- **The forecast meaning tables are asserted by existence, not value** (subtract-core-2, variety-tests-1). 5 of 5 value mutants survived, 26 of 28 WMO strings appear in no assertion, and 0 of 45 MET mappings are asserted by value. Confidence high, executed. The MET half is already tracked as A3/#89/T7.
- **Time-shaped faults and the time label have no regulator** (variety-tests-2, -3, -4; variety-runtime-2, -3, -4; tested-core-4). A drip server ran 10x past the timeout. A complete response with an open socket is reported as a timeout (exit 75). Control bytes in `time` reach stdout. A 429 is retried three times in 1.5 s. Confidence high, executed. C1 (#41, open) and A7 are known; the hold-open case, the label and the content are new.
- **Merging is not gated by checks** (variety-ci-1, variety-process-1, -2). The ruleset's required-check list is empty. 12 of 45 merged PRs had no completed green Checks run on the final head. One break reached main (Policy, fixed by #144). Confidence high, executed (gh queries). The analysts could not tell whether this is deliberate.
- **Dead or duplicate code, with real delete tests.** All three suites stayed green with these removed:
  - `FetchOpts::dump`, `log_set_level`, the two path wrappers, `edit_name`, `started_`, `Model::start`, and the ModalClock clamp (executed);
  - `WindowActions` Metric/Imperial/ToggleAuto/Location branches, dead in production and tested only as a shadow path (skeptic confirmed twice, read);
  - `load_config`, with no production caller (skeptic: partly, read).

  Each item is worth tens of lines at most.
- **Some "nothing breaks" results are test gaps, not dead code.** These are live: the `done[16]` guard (ASan overflow without it), the 429 retry, the stdout check, SIGPIPE, the address loop, request-too-long and entropy refusal. Confidence high, executed.
- **The least-measured area is OS 9 and X11.** The OS 9 TCP glue, event dispatch and xweather input have no host test, and the OS 9 target is not compiled per PR. Findings here are read-only. Skeptics narrowed three of them, and no wrong branch was found by reading. Confidence medium.
- **Much apparent complexity is justified.** This covers the seams, the hand-rolled number/JSON/HTTP code, SeedPool, DnsResolver and the weather_art Canvas template (read, high). The skeptic pass refuted nothing: of 16 checked findings, 7 were confirmed and 9 narrowed.

### variety-runtime (fetch, retry, model)
