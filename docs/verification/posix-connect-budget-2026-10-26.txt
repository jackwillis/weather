Issue #72 partial, in #41: POSIX connection-stage budget

Start a checked monotonic budget before getaddrinfo. Charge elapsed lookup time
when it returns, and share the remaining timeout across all address attempts,
including immediate failures, nonblocking waits and late immediate success.
The connection-stage budget is timeout_s; the outer fetch budget remains the
existing3x timeout_s across raw/TLS I/O. Cancel is checked before lookup, on its
return, between addresses and during sliced connect waits. Timestamp sums and
32-bit millisecond overflow are avoided. Preserve poll errno across clock reads.

CRITICAL LIMIT: this does NOT interrupt a blocked libc getaddrinfo. DNS may
still exceed both budgets until it returns, and Ctrl-C cannot yet interrupt
that call. #72 and #53 C6 remain open. No resolver worker, libc override or
production test environment switch is added. OS9 code and menu credit unchanged.

Scoped outer-budget follow-up (supersedes the shorter-explicit-limit gap above)

Net carries an optional borrowed TimeBudget. fetch_once installs a stack-owned
budget for the earlier of its attempt limit and an existing caller budget;
a scope guard restores the old pointer on success and every failure. Net.ctx
is untouched. Checked subtraction avoids timestamp addition and overflow.
POSIX connect and socket poll waits use the same Net monotonic clock and clamp
their own wait to the remaining outer budget, checking again after poll. An
expired or invalid budget refuses lookup before invoking libc. OS9 currently
ignores this optional platform budget; its raw-I/O deadline checks still apply.
Blocked POSIX getaddrinfo remains uninterruptible until it returns.
