# What It Be Like? 0.0.1 (pre-release)

A deliberately rough pre-release, published to prove the release pipeline (packaging, artifacts, tag, draft
release). It promises nothing: no support, and Mac OS 9 needs manual setup. The real gate is v0.1 (issue #45).

## What is in it
* `weather-0.0.1.tgz`: NetBSD/macppc binary package, installable with `pkg_add` (`weather`, `xweather`, man pages, LICENSE and THIRD_PARTY.md under `share/doc/weather`).
* `Weather-0.0.1.dsk`: Mac OS 9 app image with the `Weather` window app, LICENSE, THIRD_PARTY.md and a Read Me.
* Source tarball `weather-0.0.1.tar.gz` and `SHA512SUMS`. The source is the matching tag of the repository.

Free software: GNU General Public License, version 3 or (at your option) any later version. No warranty.
Weather data comes from Open-Meteo and MET Norway (CC BY 4.0); credits are in `weather --credits` and the About boxes.

## What works
* `weather`: current conditions over certificate-verified TLS from Open-Meteo and MET Norway; retry, fallback.
* `xweather`: CDE-look X11 window with a Location dialog, About and Credits pages, and automatic refresh.
* Mac OS 9 `Weather`: native window, Location dialog and persistent Preferences, manual and automatic refresh,
  About and Credits pages. The NetBSD package was installed, run and removed on a Power Mac G4.

## Known issues
* **Mac OS 9 needs a Linux (or other Unix) computer for setup.** It refuses TLS until a seed is provisioned from
  such a machine (`docs/OS9-SEEDS.md` in the source). Removing this is planned for v0.1 (#120).
* The Mac OS 9 About box layout and the `xweather` About and Credits pages have been checked in tests and on one
  machine only; `xweather` has not had a full review on maul's real X display (#70).
* One location, current conditions only.
* Open-Meteo works out of the box; MET Norway needs `contact` in the configuration, and Weather does not yet send
  `If-Modified-Since` as MET Norway's terms ask (#128).
* Open-Meteo's free tier is for non-commercial use; Weather has no ads or subscriptions.
* Nothing has been tested on hardware other than maul (Power Mac G4).
* The Open Transport import libraries linked into the OS 9 app rest on the Retro68 author's stated belief that they
  carry no copyright restrictions (`THIRD_PARTY.md`).
